HOW TO TAILOR (bank about 3 minutes per application)

1. Replace [Hiring Manager] with a real name. If unknown: "Dear Hiring Team".
2. Replace [Role Title] with the EXACT title from the job post.
3. In paragraph 3, add one sentence that mirrors a requirement from the job ad,
   using their wording (e.g. "Your mention of event-driven architecture maps to
   the real-time WebSocket streaming work I do at Miew.").
4. Change the closing paragraph to name one specific thing about them.
5. Keep it to ONE page. Never send the same letter twice.
